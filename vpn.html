YOUR DEVICES · YOUR INSTALLATION

Connect from anywhere.

Your engine stays on your PC. A private VPN lets your own devices reach its password-protected dashboard. Same-PC access works without a VPN.

Four steps

  1. Create your own VPN account. Install Tailscale on your trading PC and phone. Sign those devices into your same account. Your friend uses their own account and devices. Complete any sign-in, HTTPS or device approvals yourself.
  2. Serve your local dashboard privately. On the trading PC, open PowerShell and run the command below. Review the address it returns with tailscale serve status. Follow the official Serve instructions if Tailscale requests setup.

tailscale serve --bg --https=443 http://127.0.0.1:8765

  1. Set your private address. In AutoTrader, stop the engine, open Setup / CoinSpot connection and continue to the final step. Enter the exact HTTPS address returned by Serve, ending in .ts.net, without a trailing slash. Finish setup to restart the paper engine.
  2. Pair your other device. Select Copy private pairing link in AutoTrader. Open the link on your VPN-connected phone, check the address and open the dashboard. Sign in using this installation’s dashboard password.

Access stays private

Keep other owners in separate private networks. Do not share devices or invite other people unless you intend to grant access. Keep the application password enabled. Use Serve for private access; Funnel publishes to the internet and is outside this setup. No router port forwarding is needed.

The pairing link contains an address only. Never put a CoinSpot API key, API secret or dashboard password into a link or this public website. Enter CoinSpot keys only in the native desktop setup.

Keep the PC available

The background engine continues after closing its control window or browser. Keep Windows signed in and the PC awake; locking the screen is fine. Signing out, sleeping or shutting down stops execution. Remote access also needs an internet connection. Trading in this release is simulated; read-only account checks do not enable real orders.

Return to local-only access

Stop the engine, set http://localhost:8765 in Setup, then restart. Remove only this Serve listener with tailscale serve --https=443 off. Avoid resetting unrelated VPN configuration.

If access fails, confirm both devices are signed into your own VPN, the PC engine is running and the HTTPS address matches Setup exactly. Then check tailscale serve status. Each owner is responsible for their VPN account, plan and access settings.
